# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Businesses looking for IT consulting, systems integration, or specialist technology talent.
- IT candidates exploring specialist recruitment opportunities.
- Retail customers interested in the planned Mavenix retail offering.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces Mavenix's IT consulting, systems integration, quality engineering, and specialist recruitment services, shares information about its planned retail offering, and helps visitors contact the team or register retail interest.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current public-facing brand is Mavenix.
- Preserve the existing service offering, company history established in 2017, support email (`support@mahven.com.au`), and retail plans while rebranding.
- Contact and retail-interest forms open prefilled emails. The website does not collect or store submissions.
- The FAQ assistant uses prewritten answers in the page; it does not call an AI service or collect visitor messages.
- The site uses a locally compiled Tailwind stylesheet.

## Brand Commitments

- Use Mavenix as the public-facing company and website name throughout the site and brand assets.
- Retain existing company facts and contact details; the legal entity name and any future email/domain changes are unspecified.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current service descriptions, company history, retail plans, and FAQ content are in `index.html`.
- Current brand assets are `logo.svg` and `favicon.svg`.
- No customer testimonials, case studies, or other independent proof assets are established here; do not invent them.
